11-Year Epidemiological Study DOI Link

Tracking a Change of Trends in Spinal Cord Injury in Bangladesh (2011 to 2021)

Conducted an 11-year study of 3,575 SCI cases at Bangladesh's largest rehabilitation center. Key findings revealed 93% traumatic injuries (falls from height & RTCs), spinal TB/Pott's disease leading non-traumatic cases, and pressure sores as primary mortality cause. Utilized special modeling to reveal density of SCI across divisions in Bangladesh.
